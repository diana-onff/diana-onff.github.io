Repository

Het KMZ gaat naar de private bron-repo, de app en de data staan in de publieke. Laat je dit veld leeg, dan gaat alles naar de app-repo — dat is de oude opzet.

Een token in de browser is leesbaar voor iedereen die aan dit toestel kan. Gebruik een fine-grained token dat alleen déze repository mag, alleen inhoud en pull requests, met een korte vervaldatum. Niet doen op een gedeelde computer.

Bestand uploaden

Elk land heeft zijn eigen map in de bron-repo, zodat een nieuw bestand alleen het vorige van datzelfde land vervangt.

Het bestand komt in de wachtruimte van de bron-repo. Daarna draait de omzetting en komt het resultaat als pull request met preview te staan. Pas jouw publicatie zet het live en verplaatst het bronbestand naar source/.

Privacy

  
What Diana knows, what it doesn't, and why.


  

    
About Diana

    
Diana is a volunteer project, built as a hobby, to support WWFF-ONFF activity for radio amateurs. It is not an official WWFF service; it simply uses their public services. Diana is not a commercial product and may not be used for commercial purposes.

  


  

    
In short

    
    
      
  • Your station details, locator and settings stay in the browser of this device. Nothing is uploaded.
    • 
      
  • Your GPS position is only used on the screen in front of you. It is never sent anywhere.
    • 
      
  • Spotting yourself sends your callsign, frequency, mode and reference to WWFF Spotline, never your position.
    • 
      
  • With your separate permission, Diana can count an anonymous visit. Nothing is collected unless you say yes, and you can change your mind at any time under Settings.
    • 
      
  • Diana sets no advertising cookies and builds no profile of you.
    • 
    

  


  

    
Location on this device

    
Diana always asks your browser for the most precise position it can give. Whether that is your exact position or a deliberately blurred approximate one is decided by you, in your browser or phone settings. No website can overrule that choice.

    

    
  


  

    
What stays on this device

    
Your callsign, portable callsign, locator, and settings such as bands, the "nearby" distance, quick-reply chips and language all live in this browser's local storage, together with your statistics choice and whether you have seen the welcome screen. If you use the Beheer/Admin screen with your own GitHub access token, that token is stored the same way, on this device only. Diana also keeps an offline copy of the map tiles and of the app itself, so it keeps working without a connection; that is ordinary browser cache, not personal data.

    
  


  

    
Services Diana talks to

    
    
      
  • WWFF Spotline (spots.wwff.co): delivers live spots and agenda entries, and receives your callsign, frequency, mode, reference and remark when you spot yourself. It never receives your position.
    • 
      
  • OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org): supplies the map tiles, built from OpenStreetMap data. An ordinary request for map images; Diana sets no cookies for this.
    • 
      
  • The Diana Spotline relay (a Cloudflare Worker): passes your spot or agenda entry through to Spotline. It counts requests per hashed IP address to prevent abuse, and never logs your callsign, position or remark.
    • 
      
  • Umami (cloud.umami.is): counts an anonymous visit, only with your permission. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no personal data.
    • 
      
  • GitHub (api.github.com): only reached if you use the Beheer/Admin screen with your own access token, to publish new source files. Ordinary use of Diana never touches this.
    • 
    

  


  

    
Anonymous statistics

    
Umami is a privacy-oriented tool: it sets no cookies and builds no profile. Even so, European law treats any script that reads information from your device as something that needs your permission, cookies or not. So Diana asks first, and only loads Umami after you say yes. You can change your answer at any time in Settings.

  


  

    
What a self-spot sends

    
Reporting yourself active sends exactly five things to WWFF Spotline: the activator's callsign, the spotter's callsign, the frequency, the mode, the reference, and an optional remark you typed yourself. Nothing else, and never your position.

  


  

    
Legal basis

    
    
      
  • Showing your position on this device: your own action, through the permission your browser asks on Diana's behalf. It is never transmitted.
    • 
      
  • Sending a spot or agenda entry: carrying out the request you made by tapping send.
    • 
      
  • Anonymous statistics: your consent, freely given, and withdrawable at any time under Settings.
    • 
    

  


  

    
How long

    
Settings on this device remain until you clear them, uninstall Diana, or clear your browser data yourself. Spots and agenda entries live on WWFF Spotline, under their own retention rules, not Diana's. Statistics, if you enabled them, are handled by Umami Cloud under their own terms; Diana keeps none of it itself.

  


  

    
Outside the European Union

    
If you agreed to statistics, Umami Cloud may process that anonymous data outside the European Union, depending on their own infrastructure. Their privacy notice describes the safeguards they use.

  


  

    
Your rights

    
Because Diana keeps your personal settings only on your own device, exercising most of your rights, access, correction, deletion, is as simple as opening this page and using the button above. For anything about the anonymous statistics, or a complaint, you can reach us at diana.faunaflora@outlook.com.

    
You can also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority: Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be.

Your location

    
Diana uses your device's GPS to show which nature area you are in and to find WWFF references nearby. Your position is used only on the screen in front of you; it is never sent to a server.

    
    

    
Anonymous statistics

    
With your permission, Diana can count visits with a privacy-friendly tool, so we know the app is actually being used. No account, no profile, no tracking across other websites, and nothing at all unless you choose yes.

    
    
    
🔒 You can change this any time under Settings → Privacy.
